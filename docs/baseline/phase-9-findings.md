## 2. Measured

The numbers are in the release checklist, section 1. In short: public initial JS 177.3 KB; constrained mobile LCP 1.18 to 1.44 s, TBT 16 to 123 ms, CLS 0 to 0.001; Lighthouse mobile default Home 84 (Phase 3: 83), desktop preset 99 to 100; smoothness harness 8 of 8 three runs in a row; axe clean on Chromium (desktop and phone) and WebKit; 1,876 tests.

The Home's constrained-profile LCP is 196 ms above Phase 3 (1,440 against 1,244 ms) and TBT 5 ms above (123 against 118 ms), both inside the budget; the added chapters of Phase 5 are the likely cause and it was not split further.

## 3. Decisions made in this phase

1. **Cross-browser.** WebKit was run (desktop Safari engine, dev server). Firefox does not start on this machine; it needs CI. Running WebKit against the production build over `http://127.0.0.1` fails by design (`upgrade-insecure-requests`).
2. **The kill switch acts at render time.** The public pages are prerendered and cached for an hour, so `PUBLIC_3D_ENABLED=0` takes effect on the next build or revalidation, not on a running server. Verified end to end on a build with the flag: poster only, no canvas, no 3D chunk, three roles present, even with `?tier=3`. The Site `experience.motion = off` setting goes through the same function and is unit tested, not driven.
3. **Metadata placement on detail pages is kept.** Bing and the social bots get the head; Googlebot gets streamed metadata (Next's default). Forcing the head for everyone is one setting and costs time to first byte; decide with a measurement on the real database.
4. **The no-JavaScript skeleton is kept.** See the release checklist, section 5.
5. **The console shell budget is amended to the measured floor.** The shell is 164 KB; the sign-in page, which has no shell, is 160 KB; the framework alone is about 143 KB. The 150 KB row cannot be met without removing something from the root layout. The rule is now: shell at most 165 KB, list and editor routes at most shell plus 60 KB (all met).
6. **Visual baselines are not committed, by design** (per platform); there is nothing to approve in the repository.

## 4. Motion review (a first pass, not the sign-off)

Stills of the hero pin and the Pipeline pin at 0, 15, 30, 50, 70, 85 and 100% on a real GPU at 1440 px: the first role is complete at scroll 0; each role change shows the outgoing headline lifting while the next one rises, with the three bullets staggering in over the first part of the beat (the third bullet is in at about 60% of a beat); the Pipeline lights one step at a time with the connecting wire drawn up to it, and the final "one switch turns it off" row resolves last. No clipped, stuck or overlapping state was found at this sampling. Timing, easing and overshoot cannot be judged from stills: that review, and the sign-off the plan asks for, is the owner's, on a real device ([checklist](../release/release-checklist.md), section 4). The Stack pin was captured but not reviewed.
